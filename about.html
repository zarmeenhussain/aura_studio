Brand Philosophy

The Atelier Ethos

Born between Paris and Tokyo, AURA STUDIOS investigates the harmony between monumental Brutalist architecture and wearable luxury tailoring.

Our Manifesto
"True luxury is not an excess of embellishment, but the courage of absolute restraint."

Founded in 2022, AURA STUDIOS arose as an antidote to disposable fast-fashion and hyperbolic logos. We believe garments should function as architectural shelters for the modern human condition—structured, enduring, and meticulously calibrated in balance, proportion, and texture.

Textile Innovation

Custom Weaves & Heavyweight Gauges

We do not procure stock commercial rolls. Every fleece, poplin, and gabardine is yarn-dyed and woven exclusively for our silhouette patterns in family-run mills located in Guimarães, Portugal and Biella, Italy.

From our signature 500 GSM loopback cotton to water-repellent brushed wools, each piece is engineered with dense stitch densities and enzyme washes that deliver an exquisite, broken-in hand feel from day one.

Commitment To The Earth

Conscious Creation

We maintain limited production runs to guarantee zero deadstock incineration, supporting a circular ecosystem for generations to come.

Carbon-Neutral Logistics

Every courier flight and regional delivery is measured and offset through Gold Standard reforestation initiatives in verified biomes.

Biodegradable Packaging

Our parcels ship in water-soluble FSC-certified recycled boxes and 100% home-compostable garment envelopes. Zero petroleum plastics.

Fair Wages & Ethics

Our pattern cutters and master tailors receive wages 35% above regional minimums with comprehensive health coverage and humane studio environments.
