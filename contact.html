Client Questions & Policies

Everything you need to know about purchasing, shipping, and fabric maintenance.

Our silhouettes are intentionally designed with a contemporary, boxy, drop-shoulder cut. If you prefer the modern relaxed street drape pictured in our lookbooks, take your normal size. For a closer, more tailored fit, we suggest ordering one size down.
All orders are dispatched via DHL Express or FedEx Priority within 24 business hours from our central logistics facility in Porto, Portugal. European orders arrive in 1-2 business days; North America in 2-3 business days; Asia & Australasia in 3-4 business days.
Yes. All shipments to the United States, European Union, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and Japan are dispatched on a DDP (Delivery Duty Paid) basis. No surprise courier clearance fees will be charged at your doorstep.
We accept unworn, unwashed returns in original packaging within 30 days of parcel receipt. Prepaid courier return labels are included directly inside your package for hassle-free worldwide exchanges and refunds.
